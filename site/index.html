04 / Get started

Less setup.
More building.

From download to Discord in three simple steps. No installer, no signup, no detour through a settings maze.

  1. 01

    Grab the .exe

    Download discodex.exe from the latest release. One small file, built natively for Windows.

  2. 02

    Put it in your tray

    Run it. Discodex creates its configuration automatically and settles into the notification area.

  3. 03

    Get back to work

    Keep Discord Desktop open. Your presence connects and reconnects automatically as you build.
